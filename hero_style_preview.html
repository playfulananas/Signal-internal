SIGNAL — HERO CARD & ZONE STYLES · LOCKED
Chosen styles, now living in css/game.css as .hero-card and .hero-placed — this page renders those real classes, so it can't drift from the game. Heroes have two states like units do: a card at 92×126 (same footprint as .hand-card) and a placed form at 112×50 inside the 120×58 Hero Zone slot.

1 · CARD — 92 × 126 · .hero-card

Gold rank band marks heroes at a glance. Appears in the deck builder, hero picker and detail preview — note heroes are never in hand during play, since they're a separate roster and aren't shuffled into the 30-card deck. Powers too long for the card clamp with an ellipsis; the full text belongs in the left preview panel on hover, the same way units surface ability text.

2 · PLACED — 112 × 50 in the 120 × 58 zone · .hero-placed

112px wide so a placed hero lines up exactly with the board tiles in its column. Full power text sits behind the ⓘ hover pip (same pattern as unit abilities). Gold glow = activation still available this turn; dimmed = already spent.
States — ready · spent · empty slot · P2 colouring

3 · IN CONTEXT

Rendered with the real .hero-zone-strip / .hero-zone-slot classes from the game — P2's strip above the board, P1's below, each slot aligned to its column. Hover a pip: P2's tooltips open downward so they don't run off the top of the screen.
